Three-Cup Chicken

Sticky, glossy, basil-scented chicken in a soy–sesame oil–rice wine glaze.

Prep 15 minCook 30 min
Servings

Ingredients

Main

    Sauce

      Method

      1. Cut the chicken into bite-size pieces and pat dry. Slice the ginger, smash the garlic.

        Move on when: chicken surface feels dry to the touch.

      2. Heat sesame oil in a claypot or wok over medium heat. Fry ginger slices.

        Move on when: edges are curled and golden, about 2–3 minutes.

      3. Add chicken skin-side down and sear, turning occasionally. Add garlic and chilies for the last minute.

        Move on when: chicken is golden on all sides and the garlic is fragrant, about 6–8 minutes.

      4. Pour in soy sauce, rice wine and sugar. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer on low.

        Move on when: chicken is cooked through (no pink at the bone, 75°C / 165°F) and the sauce has reduced by about two-thirds, about 15 minutes.

      5. Uncover, turn heat to high and toss constantly.

        Move on when: sauce is thick and glossy and clings to the chicken, about 2 minutes.

      6. Turn off the heat, stir in basil leaves until just wilted, and serve hot with rice.

        Done when: basil is wilted and the aroma is strong.
